STORE POLICY: RETURNS AND REFUNDS

1. Return Window:
- Most products are eligible for return within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery.
- Electronics, mobile phones, and laptop accessories must be returned within 5 calendar days of delivery.
- Apparel and fashion items have a 10-day return policy provided tags remain intact and unworn.

2. Non-Returnable Items:
- Consumables, innerwear, personal hygiene products, and software licenses cannot be returned once opened or delivered.

3. Refund Process & Timelines:
- Refunds are initiated immediately once the returned item is picked up and passes warehouse quality inspection.
- Credit/Debit Card & Net Banking refunds reflect within 3 to 5 business days.
- UPI & Store Credit refunds reflect within 24 to 48 hours.
- For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, users must provide bank account details during the return request. COD refunds are transferred via IMPS within 3 business days.
