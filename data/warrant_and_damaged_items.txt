STORE POLICY: WARRANTY & DAMAGED/DEFECTIVE GOODS

1. Damaged or Defective Items Received:
- If a package arrives damaged, tampered with, or missing items, the user must raise a support ticket within 48 hours of delivery.
- Users may be asked to provide unboxing photos or videos to support the ticket review.
- Upon ticket approval, a replacement unit is dispatched within 24 hours, or a full refund is issued.

2. Brand Warranty Claims:
- Products sold come with official manufacturer warranty (typically 1 year for electronics and appliances).
- For functional defects occurring after the 7-day return window, users must visit the brand's authorized service center along with the purchase invoice.
- Purchase invoices can be downloaded directly from the account order history.
