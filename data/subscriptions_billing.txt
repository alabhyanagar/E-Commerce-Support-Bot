HelloQ Subscriptions, Billing & Perks Guide

[HelloQ Gold & Premium Tiers]
- HelloQ Gold includes: Unlimited Likes, Rewind last swipe, 5 free Super Likes per day, Passport mode (change location globally), and See Who Liked You.
- HelloQ Platinum includes all Gold features + Priority Likes (messages appear first in your match's queue) and seeing message read receipts.

[Super Likes & Perk Management]
- Super Likes refresh every 24 hours at 00:00 UTC. If your daily Super Likes did not credit properly after a renewal or purchase, you can use the automated account restore tool via support.
- How to restore purchases: Go to Settings > Account > Restore Purchases, or ask the support assistant to trigger an automatic account sync and perk refresh.
