STORE POLICY: SHIPPING, DELIVERY & CANCELLATIONS

1. Order Tracking & Statuses:
- Placed: Order confirmed and passed to the seller.
- Processing: Packed and ready for courier pickup.
- In Transit: Handed over to courier partners (e.g., Ekart, Delhivery, BlueDart) with an active tracking link.
- Out for Delivery: Courier agent is delivering the parcel today before 8:00 PM.

2. Order Cancellation Rules:
- Customers can cancel an order anytime before it changes to "Out for Delivery" status.
- Once an order is marked as "Out for Delivery", it cannot be cancelled online. The user must refuse delivery when the delivery partner arrives.
- If an order is cancelled prior to shipping, pre-paid amounts are refunded instantly to the original payment method.

3. Delivery Fees & Expedited Shipping:
- Standard Delivery is FREE on orders above ₹499.
- A flat shipping fee of ₹40 applies to orders under ₹499.
- Express 1-Day Delivery is available for select pincodes at ₹99 extra.
