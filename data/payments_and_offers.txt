STORE POLICY: PAYMENTS, OFFERS & INVOICING

1. Payment Methods Accepted:
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking across all major banks, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- EMI options are available on major credit cards for purchases exceeding ₹3,000.

2. Coupon & Promo Codes:
- Only one promo code or bank discount can be applied per order.
- Instant bank discounts (e.g., 10% Instant Discount on HDFC/ICICI cards) are applied automatically at checkout if eligible.

3. Tax & Invoices:
- GST invoices are generated automatically for all purchases.
- Business buyers requiring GSTIN billing must input their tax identification details during checkout prior to placing the order.
